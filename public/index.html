About

About New'Gen Dental

Our practice has been part of Seymour for decades. Dr John Paul Lee has led it since 2014, caring for patients from across the Mitchell Shire and making Seymour his home.

Having made Seymour his home, Dr Lee has a particular interest in implantology and oral surgery, with a focus on providing a wider range of treatment options locally so patients can access care without always needing to travel to a major city.

We plan treatment using an intraoral scanner, OPG imaging and RayFace facial scanning, and our in-house milling machine and furnace allow certain crowns to be made here rather than sent away.

  • Intraoral scanner
  • OPG imaging
  • RayFace facial scanning
  • In-house milling machine and furnace

Services

Our Services

We offer a broad range of dental care under one roof in Seymour.

General dentistry

Check-ups and preventative care, dental hygiene, fillings, root canal therapy, extractions, wisdom teeth removal, children's dentistry, emergency dentistry, gum disease treatment, mouthguards, dentures, and digital scans and imaging.

Cosmetic dentistry

Teeth whitening, veneers, crowns and bridges, and smile makeovers.

Orthodontics

Clear aligners, including Invisalign, Spark and Angel, as well as traditional braces.

Implants and advanced dentistry

Single tooth implants, multiple implants and full arch replacement, snoring and sleep apnoea appliances, and treatment for TMJ and jaw pain.
